# Sample Calculations - Monthly Repayment Feature

## Input Scenario

**Parent Input:**
- Current Status: Student starting college (new student)
- Years of Study: 3 years
- Maintenance Allowance: £5,000/year

**Annual Costs (Standard England):**
- Tuition Fees: £9,250/year
- Accommodation: £6,000/year (term-time only)
- Total with Maintenance: £9,250 + £6,000 + £5,000 = £20,250/year

---

## Step 1: Calculate Total Loan Amount

```
Total Loan = Annual Cost × Years of Study
Total Loan = £20,250 × 3 = £60,750
```

**Loan Breakdown:**
- Tuition: £9,250 × 3 = £27,750
- Accommodation: £6,000 × 3 = £18,000
- Maintenance: £5,000 × 3 = £15,000
- **TOTAL: £60,750**

---

## Repayment Plan Assumptions (Plan 2)

- Interest Rate: RPI + 3% (assume 6% for calculations)
- Repayment Threshold: £27,750/year
- Repayment Rate: 9% of income above threshold
- Maximum Repayment Period: 40 years
- Annual Salary Increment: 5%
