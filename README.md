# Student Loan Decision App

A web/mobile application designed to help UK parents make informed decisions about student loans for their children.

## Overview

This app supports two distinct user scenarios:

### 1. **New Students (College-bound)**
- Help parents and students decide whether to take student loans
- Compare scenarios with and without loans
- Calculate potential costs and benefits
- Understand loan repayment terms and conditions

### 2. **Graduated Students (Working)**
- Evaluate whether to pay off existing student loans
- Compare early repayment vs. long-term payment strategies
- Analyze tax implications and interest savings
- Plan financial strategy

## Target Audience

UK parents with:
- Children going to college (pre-university age)
- Children already graduated and working
- Need for financial guidance on student loan decisions
